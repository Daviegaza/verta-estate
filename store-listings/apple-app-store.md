# Vestra — Find Your Next Home in Kenya

**Category:** Lifestyle

## Description (EN)

Vestra is Kenya's most intuitive property platform, connecting tenants, landlords, and agents across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and beyond. Whether you are searching for your dream apartment, need to list a rental, or want to manage your property portfolio, Vestra makes it effortless.

### Find Properties You Will Love
Browse thousands of verified listings with high-resolution photos, 3D virtual tours, and accurate maps. Filter by price, location, property type, amenities, and more. Save your favourites and compare properties side-by-side.

### List with Confidence
Landlords and agents can create stunning listings in minutes. Set your price, upload photos, and publish instantly. Manage inquiries and track views from a single dashboard.

### Secure Payments
Pay rent and deposits directly through the app with M-Pesa, card, or bank transfer. Automated receipts, payment history, and instant notifications keep you in control.

### Tenant Management
Landlords can add tenants, send lease agreements, track payment status, and manage maintenance requests — all from your phone.

### Stay Connected
Real-time messaging between tenants, landlords, and agents. Get push notifications for inquiries, payment confirmations, lease renewals, and more.

### Verified & Trusted
Identity verification for all users. Property verification with on-ground checks. Your safety is our priority.

## Description (SW)

**Vestra — Tafuta Nyumba Yako Kenya**

Vestra ni jukwaa la mali isiyohamishika nchini Kenya linalowakutanisha wapangaji, wenyevi nyumba, na mawakala kote Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, na zaidi.

### Tafuta Nyumba Utakayoipenda
Vinjari maelfu ya matangazo yaliyothibitishwa kwa picha za ubora wa juu, ziara za pepe za 3D, na ramani sahihi. Chuja kwa bei, eneo, aina ya nyumba, na vistawishi. Hifadhi unazozipenda na ulinganishe nyumba kwa nyumba.

### Tangaza Kwa Ujasiri
Wenyevi nyumba na mawakala wanaweza kuunda matangazo mazuri kwa dakika. Weka bei yako, pakia picha, na chapisha mara moja. Simamia maswali na ufuate maoni kutoka kwa dashibodi moja.

### Malipo Salama
Lipa kodi na amana moja kwa moja kupitia M-Pesa, kadi, au benki. Risiti za kiotomatiki, historia ya malipo, na arifa za papo kwa papo zinakuweka katika udhibiti.

Wasiliana kwa wakati halisi kati ya wapangaji, wenyevi nyumba, na mawakala. Pata arifa za maswali, uthibitisho wa malipo, upyaji wa mkataba, na zaidi.
