## Full Description (EN)

Vestra is Kenya's most intuitive property platform, connecting tenants, landlords, and agents across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, and beyond. Whether you are searching for your dream apartment, need to list a rental, or want to manage your property portfolio from your phone, Vestra makes it effortless.

### FIND PROPERTIES YOU WILL LOVE
Browse thousands of verified listings with high-resolution photos, 3D virtual tours, and accurate interactive maps. Use smart filters to narrow by price, location, property type, bedrooms, amenities, and more. Save your favourites, compare properties side-by-side, and set alerts for new listings that match your criteria.

### LIST WITH CONFIDENCE
Landlords and agents can create stunning property listings in minutes. Upload photos, set your price, describe amenities, and publish instantly. A powerful dashboard lets you manage inquiries, track listing views, and measure engagement — all from your phone.

### SECURE PAYMENTS
Pay rent, deposits, and service charges directly through the app. Choose from M-Pesa, credit/debit cards, or bank transfer. Every transaction generates an automated receipt, and your complete payment history is always available. Never worry about lost rent receipts again.

### TENANT MANAGEMENT
Landlords can add tenants, send digital lease agreements, track payment status, schedule inspections, and manage maintenance requests — all from a single interface. Get notified when rent is due, paid, or overdue.

### STAY CONNECTED
Real-time messaging between tenants, landlords, and agents. Push notifications for new inquiries, payment confirmations, lease renewals, maintenance updates, and personalised property alerts.

### VERIFIED & TRUSTED
Every user goes through identity verification. Properties are verified through on-ground checks and document validation. Our AI-powered fraud detection system actively monitors for suspicious activity. Your safety and peace of mind are our priority.

### AI-POWERED INSIGHTS
Get valuation estimates, neighbourhood analytics, and rental yield projections powered by machine learning. Make informed decisions with data-driven market intelligence.

## Full Description (SW)

**Vestra — Tafuta Nyumba Yako Kenya**

Vestra ndio jukwaa la mali isiyohamishika nchini Kenya linalowakutanisha wapangaji, wenyevi nyumba, na mawakala kote Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, na zaidi.

### TAFUTA NYUMBA UTAKAOIPENDA
Vinjari maelfu ya matangazo yaliyothibitishwa kwa picha za ubora wa juu, ziara za pepe za 3D, na ramani sahihi. Chuja kwa bei, eneo, aina ya nyumba, vyumba, na vistawishi. Hifadhi unazozipenda na ulinganishe nyumba kwa nyumba.

### TANGAZA KWA UJASIRI
Wenyevi nyumba na mawakala wanaweza kuunda matangazo mazuri kwa dakika. Pakia picha, weka bei yako, na chapisha mara moja. Dashibodi yenye nguvu inakuwezesha kusimamia maswali na kufuatilia watazamaji.

### MALIPO SALAMA
Lipa kodi na amana moja kwa moja kupitia M-Pesa, kadi, au benki. Kila unapolipa utapokea risiti ya kiotomatiki. Historia yako yote ya malipo inapatikana wakati wowote.

### SIMAMIA WAPANGAJI
Wenyevi nyumba wanaweza kuongeza wapangaji, kutuma mikataba ya kukodi, kufuatilia malipo, na kusimamia maombi ya matengenezo kutoka kwenye simu yako.

### WASILIANA
Mawasiliano ya wakati halisi kati ya wapangaji, wenyevi nyumba, na mawakala. Pata arifa za maswali mapya, uthibitisho wa malipo, na tahadhari za nyumba mpya.
